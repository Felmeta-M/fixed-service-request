## Selected Projects

### Project 1: Fixed Service Request Portal

**Role:** Full-stack development · **Stack:** Laravel 12, React 19, Inertia.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Filament · **Duration:** 6-month project (2024 – Present)

Customer-facing portal for fixed-line service requests, surveys, subscriptions, and payments. Integrates multiple systems to provide a single, secure workflow for consumers and businesses.

**Integrations:**

- **OSS (ZTE OSS)** — Resource-check integration to verify network resource availability (e.g. port/address) before order submission; results drive eligibility and prefill in the creation flow.
- **CRM / BSS** — Integration with CRM and BSS (ECAF): subscription and survey orders, order status (BSS SELFCARE / HWBSS_Order), purchased offerings, and FOSS OrderQuery (GetCombining) for subscriber, customer, and account data.
- **Telebirr** — Payment integration: create order (signed requests, merchant order ID), notify webhook for confirmation, and reconciliation of payment status; RSA signing and secure callback handling.
- **eSignet (Fayda)** — National digital identity integration: OAuth2/OIDC-style flow (authorization, callback, token, userinfo) for secure login and KYC; PKCE and JWT client assertion.

**Outcomes:** Multi-step service creation and survey flows with validation and resume support; config-driven, logged integrations aligned with security and audit requirements; containerized deployment (Docker, Nginx) for infrastructure optimization and reliable hosting.
